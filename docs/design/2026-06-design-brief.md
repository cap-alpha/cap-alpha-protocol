# Design Brief — Pundit Ledger Visual Direction
*Filed 2026-06-03 · Author: Claude Code (agent) · Refs #1032*

---

## Context

**What the site is.** Pundit Ledger (cap-alpha.co) is a data product — a public accuracy ledger for sports media pundits. Every prediction is extracted, scored, and permanently on record. The primary users are sports bettors doing pre-bet research ("is this person reliable on trade rumors?") and fans who enjoy holding media accountable. This is not a lifestyle site, a news aggregator, or a SaaS dashboard. It is an evidence archive with a leaderboard.

**Who reads it.** Bettors arriving from search or social share, sports-media followers, and journalists. The bettor is the monetizable core: they want a number they can trust fast — "74% accurate on trade calls, 28 resolved" — not a visual experience.

**Feedback received (Issue #1032, item 8).** The owner reports: "the site has an aesthetic that is a mix of old and new" and "looks generated by AI." Separate human testers have echoed this. The owner is open to a new approach and asking why progress has stalled.

---

## What's Wrong Now — Concrete Observations

### 1. Two competing CSS systems coexist in globals.css

`globals.css` defines two distinct color systems that are in active conflict:

- **"V1 Data Editorial" tokens** (navy `#1A2744`, gold `#B8860B`, warm white `#F7F4EF`) — a light-on-dark newspaper aesthetic
- **shadcn/ui dark-mode tokens** (`--background: 240 10% 3.9%`, `--primary: emerald 142.1 76.2% 36.3%`) — a modern dark SaaS template

The navbar uses `var(--navy)` with a gold accent border. The hero uses `bg-black` with emerald. The body is `bg-canvas` (near-black). No page uses `--bg: #F7F4EF` (the warm off-white that would make the editorial tokens actually look editorial). The result is a dark site with a navy navbar that reads as two different products stacked vertically.

### 2. Emerald green reads as AI/SaaS template, not data product

The dominant accent color is Tailwind's `emerald-500` (`#10b981`). This specific color is used by default in:
- shadcn/ui green variants
- Vercel dashboard templates
- Dozens of SaaS starter kits

Occurrences in the codebase: the live indicator pill (`bg-emerald-500/10 text-emerald-400`), the primary CTA button (`bg-emerald-500 shadow-[0_0_24px_rgba(16,185,129,0.3)]`), the leaderboard accuracy color (`text-emerald-400` for >60%), the hero background glow (`bg-emerald-500/8 blur-[120px]`), Clerk auth button, hot/cold badges, pundit-page avatar gradient, and the brand name in the footer. Emerald saturated at full opacity is the color most associated with "ChatGPT-era startup" aesthetics.

### 3. The hero glow is the defining "AI-generated" tell

`web/app/page.tsx` line 20 and `web/app/verify/page.tsx` line 66 and `web/app/methodology/page.tsx` line 224 and `web/app/docs/page.tsx` line 160 all use the identical pattern:

```
absolute ... w-[700-800px] h-[320-400px] bg-emerald-500/8 rounded-full blur-[120px]
```

This large radial blur glow on a dark background is the single most recognizable visual signature of AI-generated UI. It appears on four separate pages. It contributes nothing to information density and signals "I asked an LLM to make this look cool."

### 4. Glassmorphism and backdrop-blur appear in active user-facing components

`web/components/waitlist-form.tsx`: `bg-black/60 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/10 rounded-xl shadow-2xl`
`web/components/persona-showcase.tsx`: `bg-black/50 backdrop-blur-2xl ... Card` with `bg-gradient-to-r` buttons
`web/components/navbar.tsx`: `backdrop-blur-md` on scroll
`web/components/intelligence-feed.tsx`: `backdrop-blur-md` gate overlay
`web/components/trade-intelligence-stream.tsx`: `bg-slate-950/80 backdrop-blur-md`

Glassmorphism with multiple color overlays and blurs is a signature of "designed by a generative tool told to make things look premium." A data product earns trust through density and clarity, not transparency effects.

### 5. Font weight overuse — "font-black everywhere"

`font-black` (weight 900) is used for: the headline, the top-ranked pundit name, accuracy percentages, rank numbers, the "LEDGER" label in the footer, and the CTA button label. When everything is maximum weight, nothing is. True editorial hierarchy reserves the heaviest weight for one or two elements per screen.

### 6. Color-coded accuracy (red/yellow/green) is doing the job of opacity and weight

The leaderboard uses `text-emerald-400` (>60%), `text-yellow-400` (45–59%), `text-red-400` (<45%). This is semantically correct but aesthetically noisy — three competing accent colors in a single list component on top of the orange HOT and blue COLD badges, producing five distinct colors in a small area.

### 7. Section-level inconsistency: navbar vs. body vs. footer

The navbar renders in `var(--navy)` with a gold border — a warm, editorial palette. The body below it renders in near-black with emerald accents — a cool SaaS palette. The inline footer inside `page.tsx` uses a third treatment (`border-zinc-900`, `text-zinc-400`). A user scanning top-to-bottom sees three separate color relationships. This is the specific "mix of old and new" the owner identified.

## Why We're Not Converging

**There is no single open design issue driving a direction.** An audit of all 62 open issues finds:

- 0 issues labeled `design` or `aesthetic`
- 3 issues labeled `ux` — all are [Planning] issues about user flows and bettor journeys (strategic, not visual)
- 1 issue labeled `ui` (issue #993 — a data/trust issue about methodology disclaimers, not visual design)
- 1 mega-issue (#1032) with design feedback as item 8 of 8 functional complaints

What happened: design decisions have been made PR-by-PR without a shared direction. The shadcn/ui template was installed (dark mode, emerald primary). A separate "V1 Data Editorial" token layer was added on top. Individual components were built by agents referencing neither system consistently. The result is accumulation, not direction.

**There is no vertical slice.** No single page has been fully committed to one aesthetic, validated, and used as the reference point for everything else. Without a reference page, every PR makes its own interpretation of "what this site looks like."

**The token system has no hierarchy.** `globals.css` defines tokens at three layers (editorial, shadcn semantic, L2/L3 depth) without resolving conflicts between them. Agents and PRs pull from whichever layer is handy in context.

---

## Proposed Direction

**One direction. No menu.**

**Editorial / Financial Times / The Athletic** — a serious, readable, data-first publication aesthetic. Light mode. Serif display type. Generous whitespace. Numbers as the primary visual element. One accent color used sparingly. No gradients. No glassmorphism. No glow.

The reference aesthetic is: The Athletic (sports authority), FT Alphaville (financial data accountability), and Stratechery (single-author, trust-through-prose). All three are light-mode, newspaper-influenced, and trusted precisely because they look like someone who has nothing to prove.

### Concrete design token proposal

**Color palette (light mode):**
- `--canvas: #FAF8F5` — warm off-white page background (already defined as `--bg: #F7F4EF`, use this)
- `--surface: #FFFFFF` — card / panel background
- `--ink: #1A1A1A` — primary text
- `--ink-2: #5C5C5C` — secondary text / metadata
- `--ink-3: #9A9A9A` — tertiary / timestamps
- `--border: #E5E1DA` — all dividers and card borders
- `--accent: #1A2744` — the existing navy, used for links, active states, and the single CTA color; **replaces emerald**
- `--correct: #1A7A4A` — green for correct predictions (existing `--pos`, keep)
- `--incorrect: #B91C1C` — red for incorrect (existing `--neg`, keep)
- `--pending: #92400E` — amber/brown for pending (existing `--warn`, keep)

**Typography:**
- Display: Playfair Display (already loaded) — used **only** for headlines and pundit names; weight 700 max, not 900
- Body: Source Sans 3 (already loaded) — all UI text, labels, metadata
- Mono: JetBrains Mono (already loaded) — numbers, percentages, hashes, dates

The fonts are already correct. The problem is application: `font-black` everywhere and inconsistent use of display vs. body weight.

**Type scale (rem):**
- `--text-hero: 3rem / 1.1` — page H1 only
- `--text-headline: 1.5rem / 1.3` — section headers, pundit names
- `--text-body: 1rem / 1.6` — all prose
- `--text-data: 0.875rem / 1` — tabular data, labels
- `--text-micro: 0.75rem / 1` — timestamps, counts, metadata

**Spacing:** 4-point base grid. Cards: 24px padding. Section gap: 48px. No fractional magic numbers.

**Removed entirely:**
- All `blur-[120px]` glow divs
- All `backdrop-blur` on non-modal surfaces
- All `glassmorphism` card treatments (`bg-black/60 backdrop-blur-md`)
- All `bg-gradient-to-r` on interactive elements
- Emerald (`#10b981`) as brand color — replaced by navy `#1A2744`
- `font-black` outside of single hero headline

## Concrete Next Step

**The vertical slice is `/ledger/dan_patrick`** — the pundit detail page. This page is the highest-value bettor surface (item 5 in #1032 confirms it's currently broken/degraded), and it is self-contained enough to implement the full new direction without touching shared layout components first.

The vertical-slice PR (separate, comes after this brief is approved) will:

1. **Switch the page to light mode** — `bg-canvas` (`#FAF8F5`), `text-ink` (`#1A1A1A`), white card surfaces
2. **Remove the emerald accent** — replace the reliability badge color and stat highlights with navy (`#1A2744`) for active states, keeping red/green only for correct/incorrect prediction outcomes
3. **Remove all gradients from the avatar** — replace `linear-gradient(135deg, #1A7A4A, #0a4a2a)` initials circle with a flat `#1A2744` background, white text initial

These three changes on one page demonstrate the direction. If approved, the same system rolls out to the leaderboard, then the homepage.
